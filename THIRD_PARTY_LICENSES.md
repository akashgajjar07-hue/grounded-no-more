## Original artwork

All illustrations in `docs/assets/svg/` (aircraft plan/side/front views, exploded diagrams,
rotable lifecycle, icon sprite, favicon, OG card) are original, generated by code in
`docs/assets/svg/src/` and licensed with this repository (MIT). They depict a generic, fictional
airliner and fictional components. They are not OEM drawings and not engineering data. No real
airline's name, logo, colours or livery is used.
